# Literature and priority record, updated 2026-09-10

Status: two independently obtained solutions are now public. This bounded
audit located no earlier public real geometric (23_4) construction. Wenhao Lu
reports that his draft was written September 6 and submitted as a JMM abstract
September 7; Will Strinz's public release appeared September 8. Affirmative
historical open-status evidence was verified, but absence from a search is not
evidence of absence from all publications, preprints, or unpublished work.

## Primary sources and actual dates

1. Michael Cuntz, [(22_4) and (26_4) configurations of lines](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.00927).
   Submitted 2017-05-02; journal online 2017-08-08, Ars Math. Contemp. 14
   (2018), 157–163; DOI 10.26493/1855-3974.1402.733.
   Abstract identifies n=23 as the remaining existence case. Section 2 defines
   geometric configurations over the real projective plane, with exactly k
   selected incidences per point and per line. Its complex 23 example is not
   a real construction. This supplies the target definition and dated history.
2. Berman, Gévay, Pisanski, [Eventually, geometric (n_k) configurations exist for all n](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.00045).
   Submitted 2021-03-31; [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.00045), page 2,
   explicitly describes geometric (23_4) existence as still open. The journal
   title is Connected geometric (n_k) configurations exist for almost all n,
   Art Discrete Appl. Math. 4(3) (2021), P3.14. The inspected preprint is the
   direct evidence used here; journal metadata is not treated as a later update.
3. Toufik Mansour, [Interview with Tomaž Pisanski](https://ecajournal.haifa.ac.il/Volume2022/ECA2022_S3I11.pdf),
   DOI 10.54550/ECA2022V2S3I11. Page 6 identifies 23 as the remaining case.
   Important date distinction: the volume is ECA 2:3 (2022), but page 1 states
   publication on 2021-10-15. This is affirmative October 2021 evidence, not
   an assertion newly made in 2022. The alternative kms-ks.org host was
   inaccessible behind an access challenge; the Haifa journal PDF was read.
4. Berman, Gévay, Pisanski, [On a New (21_4) Polycyclic Configuration](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v31i4p54/pdf/),
   Electronic J. Combin. 31(4) (2024), P4.54, DOI 10.37236/12405.
   [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.12992) submitted September 2023.
   Inspected introduction and conclusions concern a new 21-point configuration
   and a classification restricted to polycyclic realizations. This is a
   different result, not a 23-point resolution or a fresh explicit open-status
   statement. Its strong-realization definition agrees with our selected-set
   checks. Unequal orbit sizes in our example are allowed by the general
   configuration question; it does not require polycyclic symmetry.
5. Berman, Gévay, Pisanski, [Polycyclic Geometric Realizations of the Gray Configuration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14484),
   submitted 2025-02-20. Abstract concerns 27 points, including extra incidences
   turning one weak 27_3 realization into 27_4. This reinforces why exact
   non-incidences matter, but does not address the target case.
6. Berman, Gévay, Richter-Gebert, Tabachnikov,
   [When Grünbaum meets Poncelet: infinite classes of movable (n_4) configurations](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/forum-of-mathematics-sigma/article/when-grunbaum-meets-poncelet-infinite-classes-of-movable-n4-configurations/B3303AD0D1DE20B9AD18C5089AC085E3),
   Forum Math. Sigma 14 (2026), e104, online 2026-07-14,
   DOI 10.1017/fms.2026.10254. Inspected introductory definition, subject
   matter and relevant text search: movable Poncelet/celestial families and
   21-point context; no 23-point solution located. This is NOT recorded as
   a July 2026 declaration that 23 remains open.
7. Krapivin, Przybocki, Heule, [Toward Satisfiability Modulo Realizability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02958),
   submitted 2026-07-03. Its solved 23-point problem concerns avoiding empty
   convex hexagons and convex heptagons. It is a distinct problem, despite
   being a prominent recent search hit involving geometry and 23.
8. [Automated Symmetric Constructions in Discrete Geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.00224),
   submitted 2025-05-30. Abstract concerns Erdős–Szekeres and everywhere
   unbalanced points, not a four-incidence configuration construction.
9. Bramato, Halbeisen, Hungerbühler,
   [More configurations on elliptic curves](https://math.ch/norbert.hungerbuehler/publications/More_configurations_on_elliptic_curves.pdf).
   Inspected theorem 4 and closing questions concern (3r_s, sr_3), with three
   points per selected line. Not a (23_4) resolution. Search ingestion dates
   were not used as the document publication date.

10. Berman, Gévay, Pisanski, [New bounds on the existence of (n_5) and (n_6) configurations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.11986),
    submitted 2022-04-25, inspected v2 dated 2022-05-01. Introduction and
    results concern improving thresholds for degrees 5 and 6; the inspected
    text supplies no fresh explicit 23_4 open-status statement or solution.

11. Wenhao Lu, [Closing the existence problem for geometric (n_4)
    configurations](https://github.com/welu2027/23_4-configuration), public
    repository inspected at commit `9e5cc941d0fea571eb997beb709978d3ffa4d3c7` on
    2026-09-10. Lu independently obtains the same two configurations and an
    independent classification of the Klein-four-symmetric case. His draft
    describes a finite-field CP-SAT search, `nauty` enumeration, and exact
    Singular computations replicated in Macaulay2. The two witness checkers
    replayed successfully; the precise local comparison is recorded in
    [evidence/independent-work](evidence/independent-work/README.md). Lu reports
    the September 6--7 private timeline above; the public repository was created
    later and does not independently establish that version history.

## Search coverage and limits

Queries included notation variants `23_4`, `geometric configurations 23`,
`twenty-three configuration points lines`, `geometric configuration still open`,
date-qualified 2025/2026 queries, exact paper titles, and author combinations
Cuntz / Berman / Gévay / Pisanski. Recent author publication discovery included
the Gévay COBISS list generated March 2026, the Hannover Cuntz research portal,
DBLP, and MaRDI. Those indexes were discovery aids; mathematical descriptions
above rely on primary papers. Several broad notation queries produced mostly
unrelated results, and some direct index accesses were challenged or blocked.
No claim of exhaustive citation-index coverage is made. Primary alternatives
were followed where available, including journal PDFs rather than abstracts
copied into ResearchGate. An accidentally opened unrelated arXiv identifier
2308.12543 was discarded and supplies no evidence.

The latest verified *explicit* open-status statement before the two 2026
solutions in this audit is October 2021. Relevant papers through July 2026 were
checked without locating an earlier solution. These are different statements.
A specialist's independent review and knowledge of circulating work remain
useful checks. No publication was submitted by this audit.
